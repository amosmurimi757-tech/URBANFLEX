Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery timeframes?

We offer same-day delivery in Nairobi CBD and next-day delivery to other Nairobi areas. Delivery to other regions takes 2-3 days.

Do you accept returns?

Yes! We accept returns within 7 days if the item is unused and in original condition. Contact us for a return authorization.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking link via WhatsApp or SMS after your order is placed. You can track your delivery in real-time.

What payment methods do you accept?
